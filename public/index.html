Division I women's gymnastics

A 10.0, the moment it happens.

Sign up once. When any NCAA Division I women's gymnast scores a perfect 10 on vault, bars, beam, or floor, your phone gets a push.

  • 1alert per gymnast, per event, per meet. No repeats.
  • 10.0exactly. A 9.975 does not buzz your phone.
  • Offwhenever you want. Every notification has an unsubscribe action. No account.

Get the alerts

iPhone. Push needs iOS 16.4 or newer, and the site has to be on your Home Screen. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Perfect 10 Alerts from that icon, then tap Send me the 10s and allow notifications.

Privacy

Your push subscription lives in a private database used only to send these alerts. It is not in the public code, not sold, and not shared. Every notification has an unsubscribe action, and the link after you sign up can remove this device. No login. Scores come from public meet data. This is an unofficial fan project by The Five That Score, not affiliated with the NCAA, Virtius, or Road to Nationals.
