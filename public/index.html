How to set up

iOS 16.4 or newer, in Safari. Chrome and in-app browsers cannot add the icon.

Share then Add to Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button. On iPhone it is at the bottom of Safari. On iPad it is in the toolbar.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
  4. Open the new Perfect 10 Alerts icon.
  5. Tap Turn on alerts.
  6. Tap Allow.

Missed Allow? Open the Home Screen icon, then Settings, Notifications, Perfect 10 Alerts, and turn Allow Notifications on.

You opened this from the Home Screen icon. That is the right place. iOS 16.4 or newer.

  1. Tap Turn on alerts.
  2. Tap Allow.

Missed Allow? Stay in this icon, then open Settings, Notifications, Perfect 10 Alerts, and turn Allow Notifications on. Come back and tap Turn on alerts again.

This browser cannot turn on iPhone alerts. Safari can. iOS 16.4 or newer.

  1. Tap Copy link.
  2. Open Safari and paste the link.
  3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open the new icon, tap Turn on alerts, then Allow.

Chrome, Instagram, X, and other in-app browsers cannot add the Home Screen icon.

  1. Tap Turn on alerts.
  2. Tap Allow.

Optional: open the Chrome menu and tap Add to Home screen, then open that icon.

Blocked them? Tap the lock icon in the address bar, then Permissions, then Notifications, and choose Allow. You can also use Chrome Settings, Site settings, Notifications. Then tap Turn on alerts again.

  1. Tap Turn on alerts.
  2. Click Allow on the browser prompt.

Blocked them? Click the lock icon in the address bar, set Notifications to Allow, then tap Turn on alerts again.
