Division I women's gymnastics

A 10.0, the moment it happens.

Sign up once. When any NCAA Division I women's gymnast scores a perfect 10 on vault, bars, beam, or floor, you get a push, an email, or both.

  • 1alert per gymnast, per event, per meet. No repeats.
  • 10.0exactly. A 9.975 does not buzz your phone.
  • Offwhenever you want. Every message has a remove link. No account.

Get the alerts

iPhone. Push needs iOS 16.4 or newer, and the site has to be on your Home Screen. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Perfect 10 Alerts from that icon, then choose Push. Email works in the browser without that step.

Privacy

Your email and push subscription live in a private database used only to send these alerts. They are not in the public code, not sold, and not shared. Unsubscribe any time with no login. Scores come from public meet data. This is an unofficial fan project by The Five That Score, not affiliated with the NCAA, Virtius, or Road to Nationals.
