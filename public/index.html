How to set up

iOS 16.4 or newer. Safari only, and not a Private tab.

  1. Open this page in Safari. In X, Instagram, or Messages, tap Open in Safari first.
  2. Tap ••• next to the address bar, then Share. On older iOS, tap Share.
  3. Tap View More, or scroll, and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  5. Open the Home Screen icon, tap Turn on alerts, then Allow. Missed it? Settings, Notifications, Perfect 10 Alerts.

You opened this from the Home Screen icon. That is the right place. iOS 16.4 or newer.

  1. Tap Turn on alerts.
  2. Tap Allow.

Missed Allow? Stay in this icon, then open Settings, Notifications, Perfect 10 Alerts, and turn Allow Notifications on. Come back and tap Turn on alerts again.

This is not Safari. iPhone alerts need the Home Screen icon, on iOS 16.4 or newer, and not a Private tab.

  1. Tap Open in Safari. If you only see this page inside X, Instagram, or Messages, use that button. Or tap Copy link and paste it into Safari.
  2. Tap ••• next to the address bar, then Share. On older iOS, tap the Share square with an arrow.
  3. Tap View More, or scroll, and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  5. Open the icon, tap Turn on alerts, then Allow.
  1. Tap Turn on alerts.
  2. Tap Allow.

Optional: open the Chrome menu and tap Add to Home screen, then open that icon.

Blocked them? Tap the lock icon in the address bar, then Permissions, then Notifications, and choose Allow. You can also use Chrome Settings, Site settings, Notifications. Then tap Turn on alerts again.

  1. Tap Turn on alerts.
  2. Click Allow on the browser prompt.

Blocked them? Click the lock icon in the address bar, set Notifications to Allow, then tap Turn on alerts again.
